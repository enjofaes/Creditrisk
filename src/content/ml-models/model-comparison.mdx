## The Champion-Challenger Framework

Production credit models operate under a **champion-challenger** framework:

- **Champion**: The current production model (often logistic regression)
- **Challenger**: A candidate new model (e.g., XGBoost with SHAP)

Challengers are tested on a random sample (e.g., 5%) of new applications. If the challenger demonstrates superior risk-adjusted performance without unacceptable regulatory risk, it becomes the new champion.

## The Regulatory Trade-off

A 3–5 Gini point improvement from XGBoost over logistic regression sounds attractive, but consider the full cost-benefit:

| Dimension | Logistic Regression | XGBoost + SHAP |
|---|---|---|
| Discrimination | Lower Gini | Higher Gini |
| Interpretability | Coefficients = direct explanation | Requires SHAP post-hoc |
| Regulatory approval | Standard approval path | Extended SR 11-7 documentation |
| Model maintenance | Stable, slow to overfit | More sensitive to distribution shift |
| Audit trail | Simple | Requires SHAP infrastructure |
| Recalibration cost | Low | Moderate |

<Callout type="important" title="When to Use ML">
The ML uplift is most justified when: (1) the Gini improvement exceeds 5 points, (2) the portfolio is large enough to justify the SHAP infrastructure, and (3) the regulatory environment (jurisdiction, supervisor) is open to ML models with explainability.
</Callout>
